📋 Biomedical Waste Management Rules (CPCB & WHO)
Regulatory Protocol

🟡 Yellow Container (Soiled / Anatomical / Infectious Waste)

Accepted Items: Cotton gauze, cotton swabs, soiled surgical masks, contaminated hospital scrubs, surgical linen, discarded clinical paper, body tissues, expired medicines.

Disposal Standard: Incineration at 1050°C in double-chamber incinerators or high-temperature Plasma Pyrolysis followed by secured landfill disposal.

🔴 Red Container (Contaminated Recyclable Plastics)

Accepted Items: Nitrile & latex gloves, IV fluid bottles, intravenous catheters, urine bags, dialysis tubes, syringes without needles.

Disposal Standard: Chemical sterilization with 1-2% sodium hypochlorite / autoclaving, followed by shredding and controlled plastic recycling.

⚪ White Container (Puncture-Proof Sharps Box)

Accepted Items: Hypodermic needles, scalpels, surgical blades, contaminated needle caps, syringes with fixed needles.

Disposal Standard: Needle tip cutter at point of use, autoclaving / dry heat sterilization, followed by shredding and encapsulation in concrete.

🔵 Blue Container (Cardboard Box with Blue Marking)

Accepted Items: Glass vials, medicine ampoules, glass specimen test tubes, orthopedic metal implants.

Disposal Standard: Autoclave or immersion in 1% sodium hypochlorite disinfectant, followed by cullet glass recycling.

⚖️ Academic Prototype Notice: This software is an engineering proof-of-concept for automated vision-assisted hospital segregation. It must complement, not replace, certified clinical waste management protocols.
